How It Works

Three simple steps to request and track animal assistance.

  1. STEP 01

    Submit a Report

    Provide the animal's condition, location, and photos.

  2. STEP 02

    Report Review

    Municipal Veterinary Office staff review the submitted information.

  3. STEP 03

    Track Updates

    Check your report's current status in My Stray Reports.

Important Reminder

This reporting feature is not an emergency hotline. If someone is in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services directly.
